What needs to change

Ordered by impact on the practice experience
01

Compact the header

High

Uno234 stacks an eyebrow, title, subtitle, mascot, and separate navigation. Its desktop header is about 115px high before navigation; the other apps use 47–56px.

Use a plum wordmark, a short muted tagline, and compact navigation. Keep the brand stable between Practice and Settings; put the page heading inside the activity surface.

02

Combine the setup controls

High

The Mode / preset card, Next row, summary, and instruction banner consume separate bands of space. Shared controls take about 228px on desktop and 307px on mobile.

Bring mode, preset access, exercise summary, and Next into a compact toolbar inside one practice surface. Keep a short counting hint near the score and put extended instructions in help.

03

Keep actions near the score

High

Check / Reset follow the passage, wrapping control, explanatory copy, and another heading. Tap and Both add performance controls and technical notes to an already long page.

Group the primary action and status near the score or pad. Put review details below the working area. Preserve the reserved count-in row and stationary score / pad during performance.

04

Keep page alignment stable

Medium

Practice can expand to 1920px; Settings returns to 940px. The header and shared controls move when the view changes.

Use the shared content-width convention for header and setup. Let a wider score area be a deliberate exception. Recheck system breaks at the existing 680px and 1360px container thresholds.

05

Match the control language

Medium

Uno234 inherits white outlined controls, 12px corners, and body-font button labels from Count Writing. tUno and tuNotes use tinted controls and Nunito heading-font labels.

Apply shared shell styles: tinted controls, plum selected states, and the 16px control radius. Keep count inputs and duration / rest editing affordances specialized.

06

Move inventory into help

Medium

Practice repeats persistence and pending-setting explanations. Settings retains the setup block and planned-control inventory; performance includes prototype and latency prose.

Keep actionable errors and a concise settings explanation visible. Group implemented options by exercise, writing, and performance. Move planned work and technical detail into documentation or help.

07

Finish with a quieter footer

Lower

Use the other apps’ small, muted footer treatment. Place Uno beside the activity where space allows, rather than in the header. Installation and offline support need a separate product decision.

Keep what already works

Responsive score systems produced no horizontal overflow in the sampled layouts. The palette and bundled fonts already match the other apps.

Retain responsive wrapping, the stable count-in row, specialized notation editing, and the same score and responses across mode changes. A fixed-height play surface must be checked against four-measure exercises so counts and the pad remain accessible.

A practical order of work

  1. Align the shell

    Brand, navigation, controls, and stable page widths.

  2. Consolidate setup

    One practice surface, compact toolbar, and secondary guidance in help.

  3. Refine each mode

    Score and actions together, followed by desktop and mobile comparison screenshots.

Acceptance checks for implementation
  • All three response modes and Settings at 1536, 1280, 768, 390, and 320px.
  • One- through four-measure passages, including 6/8.
  • Stable header alignment, no horizontal overflow, legible counts, and reachable primary actions.
  • Responses retained through mode and settings changes; stationary score and pad during count-in and recording.
  • Run npm run check and relevant rhythm browser scenarios after application changes.
