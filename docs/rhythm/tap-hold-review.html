01 / Behavior

Choice without replacement

Tap · default

“Tap when each note starts.” Attacks, missing notes, extra attacks and attacks during rests receive feedback. Release and sustain do not.

Tap + Hold

“Hold each note for its length. Release for rests.” Separate notes need release and reattack. Tied chains need one continuous hold.

The setting locks during count-in and capture. Completed attempts keep their original choice and policy; retry uses the current selection. Calibration continues to use the fixed input-specific session offset.

02 / Review

Show what happened

Tap keeps attack markers. Tap + Hold adds thin bars from onset to actual release, with tied durations continuing across bars and wrapped systems.

Accepted gaps stay visible. Accepted early attacks after rests retain raw overlap without a rest violation. Matched bars start at their attack markers, including just before a barline. Early releases, overholds and unmatched holds keep their actual timing. Incomplete holds use a distinct dashed extent, no release endpoint, and “no release observed.”

Text, SVG descriptions and endpoint shapes supplement color. No live sustain animation or additional guidance mode.

03 / Provisional policy

Bounded allowances

Attack acceptance±80 ms; association remains ±180 ms
Ordinary releaseSmaller of 60 ms or 10% of sounding duration
Adjacent early releaseSmaller of 80 ms or 15% of preceding sounding duration
Minimum sustain70% of expected duration

Engineering defaults for review, not approved teaching thresholds. Ordinary and articulation allowances never add together. Next attacks are checked independently against expected onset. No student tolerance controls.
